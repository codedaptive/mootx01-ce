## Can We Support This?

**Partially.** The sync machinery ships and works. The guide covers it accurately.

Two items reduce supportability for end users:

1. **No in-app toggle (not ready, documented in guide).** Whether sync is
   enabled is a build-level decision. Users cannot turn it on or off from
   within the app. This will generate support contacts when users expect to
   find a sync setting and don't. Documented in "What is not ready yet."

2. **Tier-rise retraction (not ready, documented in guide).** A privacy
   promotion to Restricted/Secret after sync does not retract the snapshot
   from peer devices. Documented in "What is not ready yet" with the
   explicit user guidance: delete from the peer explicitly. This is the
   edge case most likely to generate a support contact and the most important
   one to have in the guide before the feature ships to users.

## Advisory Strings Documented

The following error/status strings were documented in the guide:

- `"Re-enrolling with sync"` — device re-claiming a slot after eviction or
  exceeding 15-device ceiling
- `"Held for migration"` — records from a newer app version waiting for local
  schema migration (version-skew hold)
- `"Parked entries"` — outbox entries that failed to push, queued for retry
