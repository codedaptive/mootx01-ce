## Key Outcomes

- **Sensitivity-gate recommendation VALIDATED with amendments** (shapes
  P5-M1): consumer-side SensitivityFilteredStorage wrapper + syncCeiling,
  not manifest predicates. Amendments: (a) the wrapper must be the same
  handle passed to `enable()` so integrity-hook repairs ride the gate;
  (b) decide retract semantics for rows whose tier rises after sync.
  Also flagged: content ships as standard CKRecord fields — evaluate
  `CKRecord.encryptedValues` for content columns in P5-M1.
- **TombstoneGC latent 40-bit clock bug** (TombstoneGC.swift:75): first
  production wiring would compact every tombstone instantly, reopening
  the A6 stale-resurrect window. No production caller today. Mask fix
  (mirroring the Adams P1 SlotTable fix) mandatory before wiring GC.
- **Deleted-row payload retention**: tombstone apply does not purge
  matching `_ck_pending_skew` entries or parked outbox payloads; parked
  payloads persist indefinitely. Purge-on-tombstone recommended.
- Slot registry, integrity hook, wire decode, remote-wake parsing, and
  crypto posture all hold under the same-account threat model;
  per-record quarantine and the PersistenceKit SQL-identifier guard
  (CAND-047) close the adversarial-peer decode and injection paths.
