# Validation — The Evidence Behind The Claims

Execution evidence: conformance results, performance measurements, audit
records. A claim in a specification is checkable because something here
measured it, with the date, the hardware and the revision recorded so the
measurement can be repeated.

Evidence is not a report about evidence. A narrative summary belongs in the
maintainer tree; what belongs here is the material a sceptic can re-run.

This directory publishes, and the audit ledgers are the sharpest edge in the
tree: the published ledgers are authored in `_public_static/validation/audits/`
and omit the findings whose fixes are maintainer-only. The full ledgers live in
the maintainer tree and never move here.
