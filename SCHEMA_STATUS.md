# Schema status

**Status: v1.0.0-beta — schema tentatively frozen.**

As of the 1.0.0-beta release, the schema is tentatively frozen. We consider
it stable and suitable to build against. Any change during the beta window
will be additive — new fields or capabilities — never the removal or
redefinition of anything already here.

Because this is a beta, we are not yet shipping a migration tool, and we do
not yet guarantee that data created under the beta carries forward unchanged
to the final 1.0.0 release. In practice we expect compatibility; treat the
beta as a release-candidate window rather than the final stability commitment.

When the schema is frozen at the 1.0.0 release, this notice will be replaced
by the full stability and migration commitment.
