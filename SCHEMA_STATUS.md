# Schema status

**Status: pre-1.0 — rapid development. Experimental. No migration guarantee.**

Until the 1.0 release, or until this notice is removed, the schema is under
rapid development while we work through edge cases. As of now we believe it
is stable and suitable to experiment with. However, we may alter the schema
before it is frozen, and we are not providing a migration tool during this
period. If the schema changes, data created under an earlier version is not
guaranteed to carry forward. Treat anything you build in this window as
experimental — a way to get your feet wet with the system — and do not rely
on it persisting across schema changes.

When the schema is frozen at 1.0, this notice will be removed and the
stability commitment that replaces it will be stated here.
